## Core IP/non-compete principle

Atreyu's primary concern is **concurrent and future ventures**, not pre-existing code.

The rule: CMM can only claim IP created specifically for CMM's **actively operating marketplace**. The broad "Company Business" definition (which covers boats, aircraft, motorcycles, bicycles) was written to protect CMM's expansion potential — it should not restrict Atreyu's independent work or claim his outside projects.

Non-compete is limited to what CMM is **actually operating at the time** — not the aspirational scope in the definition. Automotive software, aviation, marine, motorcycles, and any market CMM hasn't entered are all free.
